Learn Docker and Kubernetes by breaking and fixing them

A 25-lesson, scenario-based course that takes you from your first container to debugging a GitOps deployment. Every lesson drops you into a simulated terminal with a real situation: something to build, or something broken to diagnose and repair. Nothing to install, nothing you can damage.

🎯 The goal of this course

To make you the person who can fix it. By the end you should be able to explain how containers, Kubernetes, Helm and Argo CD work, read the evidence a broken system gives you (statuses, events, logs, exit codes), find the cause with a repeatable method, and apply the right fix in the right place.

Every lesson starts with its own goal and why it matters, and ends with scenarios, key takeaways and a quiz.

Prerequisites You should be comfortable in a terminal (cd, ls, cat) and able to read YAML. You need no Docker or Kubernetes experience and no local installation: the sandbox is a simulation that runs entirely in this page. It reproduces the commands, output and failure modes of the real tools closely, but it is not the real thing; the notes on the simulator list what is and is not modelled.
How to get the most out of this course
  1. Type the commands yourself. The sandbox has history (↑) and completion (Tab), so it is quick.
  2. In a fix-it scenario, resist the solution. Read the status, then the events, then the logs, and form a guess before you change anything.
  3. Use the hints one at a time. When you do open a solution, read the "why this works" box: that is the part that transfers to real systems.
  4. Stuck on a message? Select it in the terminal, right-click, and ask the AI tutor what it means.
  5. Once a scenario is solved, press Reset and break it differently. The sandbox is free: use it to try things you would not dare on a real cluster.
